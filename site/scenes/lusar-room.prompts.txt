Implementation
The two aligned frames are used for an illustrative lighting preview. Soft masks isolate each fixture and its light pool. This is not a photometric calculation and does not claim that the illustrative fixtures are catalog products. Each circuit has independent brightness and an approximate warm-to-neutral temperature preview. WebP exports are the deployed assets; original generated PNGs remain in the local generation history.
